# Code of Conduct

Loopchii public collaboration should feel rigorous, welcoming, and useful.

## We Expect

- respect for contributors with different levels of expertise
- precise, evidence-oriented feedback
- patience with questions asked in good faith
- public discussion that improves the work instead of escalating conflict

## We Do Not Allow

- harassment, hostility, or personal attacks
- prejudice or exclusionary behavior
- spam, baiting, or performative bad-faith argument
- pressure to disclose private or proprietary system details

## Resolution

If a conversation stops being constructive, maintainers may close, redirect, or remove it.

Questions or concerns can be sent to [hello@loopchii.com](mailto:hello@loopchii.com).
