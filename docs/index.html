Why we build

The interesting part
is rarely only technical.

We are LOOPCHii, an independent research and software company exploring consequential AI: intelligence whose decisions can affect people’s opportunities, care, surroundings and choices.

A system can be capable and still be difficult to question. An interface can be beautiful and still leave someone behind. We bring research, software and design together to ask what those gaps mean—and what deserves to be built differently.

Explore our research questions ↗
Academic fields have useful boundaries.
Real consequences rarely respect them.

We follow connections between people, institutions and environments without assuming that every connection is already an answer.

◎ A number is a beginning.+

A result becomes more useful when we can ask what it represents, what it leaves out, and whose experience it describes.

⌁ Context changes the question.+

A decision that looks simple on screen can have a different meaning in a hospital, a workplace, or a neighborhood. That difference is worth understanding.

✧ Wonder has practical uses.+

A visual model or a moment of play can help someone notice what a paragraph missed. Design is part of how we make room for understanding.
