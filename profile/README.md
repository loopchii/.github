### Questions worth building around

We are an independent research and software company exploring **consequential AI**: intelligence whose decisions can affect people’s opportunities, care, surroundings and choices. We bring research, software and design together because a technically capable system still has to make sense in a human life.

Academic fields have useful boundaries. Real consequences rarely respect them. A question about technology can also be a question about an institution, an environment, or who gets to say “that isn’t right.” We follow those connections without assuming that every connection is already an answer.

We like data, 3D worlds and questions that refuse to stay in one discipline. In high-risk settings, that curiosity comes with responsibility: evidence should be open to challenge, uncertainty should remain visible, and people should have a say in what affects them.

Serious work does not require a joyless world. We checked.

### A little world. Absolutely no obligation to be normal.

**Ouchii Mori** brings a different kind of question into the day: *what if beginning felt a little more like exploring?* There is room for small steps, peculiar discoveries, and a pace that belongs to you.

Planned for **iOS, Mac, Apple Watch and Google Play**. Visit the [game preview](https://www.loopchii.games/) or write to [ouchiimori@loopchii.com](mailto:ouchiimori@loopchii.com).

### Something you can open, something you can ask

[**Stream**](https://github.com/loopchii/Stream) is our public media-analysis project. Read its documentation for scope, examples and limitations, or follow the [contribution guide](https://github.com/loopchii/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) to take part. A thoughtful issue, a clearer example, an accessibility fix: useful work has more than one entry point.

For a research collaboration or a focused software or design project, [tell us what you are trying to understand](https://www.loopchii.com/contact/). For investment conversations, [start with the company’s investor introduction](https://www.loopchii.com/investors/).
